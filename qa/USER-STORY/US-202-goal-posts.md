# US-202: Goal Posts

## User Story
As a goal owner,
I want to create posts on my goal,
So that I can share progress updates with my followers.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC-1: Owner can create posts on their goal
- [ ] AC-2: Posts have content text
- [ ] AC-3: Posts can include images
- [ ] AC-4: Posts have types: update, milestone, achievement, challenge, motivation
- [ ] AC-5: Posts can be linked to specific steps
- [ ] AC-6: Owner can edit their posts
- [ ] AC-7: Owner can delete their posts
- [ ] AC-8: Posts appear in goal feed

## User Types
- Registered User (Goal Owner)

## Priority
High
