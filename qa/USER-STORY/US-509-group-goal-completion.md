# US-509: Group Goal Completion

## User Story
As a coach,
I want to track and close completed group goals,
So that I can measure success and archive finished programs.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] AC-1: Coach can see overall group completion %
- [ ] AC-2: Coach can mark group goal as completed
- [ ] AC-3: Individual participant statuses are preserved
- [ ] AC-4: Completed group goals move to history
- [ ] AC-5: Statistics and summary available
- [ ] AC-6: Participant instances remain in their history

## User Types
- Coach
