Questions

Common Questions

Why 66 days? ▼
Research from University College London shows it takes an average of 66 days to form a lasting habit — not 21, not 30. The challenge is broken into three phases: Destruction (breaking old patterns), Installation (building new routines), and Integration (making it automatic). By Day 66, your new habits are locked in.
Is it free? ▼
Yes — tracking your challenge, daily tasks, mood, and reflections is completely free. Premium features like detailed analytics and advanced community tools may be added in the future, but the core challenge experience will always be free.
What happens if I fail a day? ▼
It depends on your settings. In strict mode (0 failed days allowed), any missed day resets your challenge. But you can configure a grace period — allowing 1, 2, or more failed days. Either way, you can always start a new attempt. Most finishers didn't succeed on their first try.
How is this different from 75 Hard? ▼
Total 66 is designed to be more flexible and sustainable. It's 9 days shorter, offers optional tasks alongside required ones, lets you build custom schedules, and includes community accountability with mood tracking. The goal isn't punishment — it's building habits that actually last beyond the challenge.
Can I create my own challenge? ▼
Absolutely. Choose the Custom preset and add your own tasks — anything from "Practice piano 30 min" to "Cold shower every morning." Set which days each task applies (every day, weekdays, or specific days). It's your challenge, your rules.
Is my challenge public? ▼
Only if you want it to be. When starting a challenge, you choose Public or Private. Public challenges appear in the community feed where friends can like and comment on your daily updates. Private challenges are visible only to you.
